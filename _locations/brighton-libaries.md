Brighton Libraries already stock a range of books on Palestine. They have many books of fiction and non-fiction, many published since the start of the 2023 genocide as well as some interesting archival resources.

### Book Search
To search the Brighton Library catalogue and reserve a book see the [Library Search Page](https://brighton-hove.spydus.co.uk/).

### Purchase Request
To request a new book purchase, go to the [Library Search Page](https://brighton-hove.spydus.co.uk/) and click the ‘suggest a title’ link on the right-hand side and complete the form.

### Books

Books available in Brighton Libraries include

- Rad, Assal - Don't say Palestine: How the media manufactured consent for genocide 
- Abdelnoor, Amy - Ever land (Fiction)
- Shehadeh, Raja - Forgotten: searching for Palestine's hidden places and lost memorials
- Khalil, Mahmoud - No land to stand on: protest, Palestine and being unfree in America
- Assadi, Hannah Lillith - Paradiso 17 (Fiction, electronic book)
- Weizman, Eyal - Ungrounding: the architecture of genocide
- Alnaouq, Ahmed (editor) - We Are Not Numbers: the voices of Gaza's youth
- Albanese, Francesca - When the world sleeps: stories, words and wounds of Palestine
- Jorie Graham (editor) - You must live: new poetry from Palestine
- Oborne, Peter - Complicit: Britain's role in the destruction of Gaza
- Aslan, Reza - A kids book about Israel & Palestine
