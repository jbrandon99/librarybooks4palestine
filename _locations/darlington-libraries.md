Darlington Libraries have been happy to purchase books on Palestine. They have not rejected any book requests in our experience, except for where a book had gone out of print and unavailable.

## Book Search
To search the Darlington Library catalogue and reserve a book see the [Library Search Page](https://www2.darlington.gov.uk/extended-search).

## Purchase Request
To request a new book purchase, email the details below to libraryrequests@darlington.gov.uk. 

- Borrower Card Number (will be on your library card)
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Book Title
- Author
- Format (Physical Book / E-Book / Audio Book)
- Collection point: Darlington / Cockerton

## Books

Darlington Libraries have a number of books on Palestine, including:

### Mornings in Jenin by Susan Abulhawa

[**Mornings in Jenin**](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6692041-mornings-in-jenin) is a heart-wrenching, powerfully written novel, spanning three generations of a Palestinian family through love and loss, war and oppression.

![Mornings in Jenin, Hollow Land and a coffee on a table](/assets/images/hollow-land-mornings-in-jenin-darlington.jpg.resized.jpg)

### Hollow Land: Israel’s Architecture of Occupation by Eyal Weizman 

In [**Hollow Land: Israel's Architecture of Occupation**](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1086651.Hollow_Land) the tunnels of Gaza to the militarized airspace of the Occupied Territories, Eyal Weizman unravels the mechanisms of control and how they have transformed Gaza and the West Bank into a war zone. It is fascinating and highly readable even as in provides a powerful in-depth lens of the occupation.

### Sambac Beneath Unlikely Skies by Heba Hayek

[**Sambac Beneath Unlikely Skies**](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/56251101-sambac-beneath-unlikely-skies?ac=1) by Heba Hayekis written for those who had to leave—collected remembrances of a childhood in Gaza by a woman far from Palestine’s sun and sea. Overindulgent, chaotic and sentimental, Heba Hayek’s narrator struggles to navigate life in colder, unfamiliar worlds. She holds tightly to memories of home, hoping they will lead back to her sisters and mothers.
