It is far easier for Israel to continue these atrocities when the world does not understand the the occupation.</strong></p>

        <h3>Why Libraries?</h3>
        <p>Put simply, the idea of free books is a radical idea, helping provide local communities with education and enjoyment for no cost. Yet in the UK libraries have been underfunded, with many closing down due to over years of austerity and cuts. </p>
        <p><strong>Libraries are easier to close down if people do not use them. By requesting books, you are actively helping keep your local libraries running.</strong></p>
        <p>Being part of this campaign means you can help both to keep your libraries open and build an educational resource on Palestine. Through this campaign we hope to: </p>
        <ul>
          <li>Increase people's knowledge about the Israeli occupation</li>
          <li>Build and improve local educational resources</li>
          <li>Reconnect people to their local libraries and communities</li>
        </ul>
